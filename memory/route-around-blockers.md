When a technique fails partway, Nick wants a workaround found and the task finished, not a
well-documented stop. Characterising a blocker precisely is necessary but it is not the
deliverable.

**Why:** 2026-08-01, Abbitt sculpt tutorial. `bpy.ops.sculpt.brush_stroke` ignored brush size in
scripted strokes (8 verts affected regardless of a 200-1200px range). I diagnosed it correctly,
then stopped and offered options, having dismissed the obvious workaround (issue many small dabs,
since each dab works at its fixed radius) because it would not be a faithful "copy" of the
instructor. Nick: "my problem is you stop the task halfway. i want you to find solutions around
the problem."

**How to apply:** when blocked, list the routes around it and TAKE one, then report what was done
and what it cost in fidelity. Purity of method is my concern to raise in a sentence, not a reason
to stop. If every route is genuinely closed, say so only after trying at least one.
