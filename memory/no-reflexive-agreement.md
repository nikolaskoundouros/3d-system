2026-07-31, said with force ("this has been a problem all week"): stop opening
replies with "you're right", "good catch", "exactly", or any affirmation
reflex. It reads as sycophancy and adds nothing.

**Why:** Nick wants real analysis, not agreement. Empty validation wastes his
time and hides whether I actually understood the problem.

**How to apply:** when he reports a defect, do NOT restate it back as a
checklist and agree. Instead: look at the artifact myself, diagnose the ROOT
CAUSE (often several reported symptoms share one cause), say something he
did NOT already know, then fix. Lead with the diagnosis or the work, never
with praise of his observation. Applies to all deliverable review
([[show-before-approve]], [[skill-of-comparison]]). If I catch myself typing
an affirmation, delete it and start with the finding.
