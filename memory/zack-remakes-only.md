Every clip we make is a remake of one existing Zack D. Films Short: take his script and video, do what he does, then alter it a bit. No from-scratch topics yet (Nick, 2026-10-05, after I wrote 5 new-topic scripts that he set aside).

**Why:** the whole system works by comparing against Zack's actual clip, beat by beat, so a clip with no Zack original has nothing to be checked against.

**How to apply:**
- Start from a picked Zack Short (≥1 year old, ranked by views, ~/Desktop/zack-shorts/data/top30_scripts.json). Download its video as the reference.
- Our script keeps his beats in his order with our own wording, plus one small verified addition. The ingrown hair VO is the model: about 75 vs 70 words, same 4 beats, plus a warm compress / infection beat.
- Fact-check his claims too. His knuckle Short says "about 30 minutes" where papers say about 20.
