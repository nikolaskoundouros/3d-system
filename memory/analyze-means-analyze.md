2026-10-15: after I analysed his "Keep thinking" reference I offered to start on our film's sound, he said "yes go", and I
built a whole new soundtrack alone. He: "we'll have to delete this version. and do it together. i guess thats the best
way. but your job is to analyze the clip i just gave you." The version was deleted.

**Why:** he wants to make the creative calls on the build himself, step by step; my part is a deep analysis he can
direct from.

**How to apply:** when he gives a reference, deliver the analysis and stop. Build only together with him, one step at a
time, each step agreed in chat. A "yes go" to a question about starting is not a brief to build the whole thing alone.
