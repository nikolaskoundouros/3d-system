Nick, 2026-11-12, on the cruiser compare sheet (storyboard beside my box-built render), after two days of the navy storyboard to Blender test: "can you stop the process, this is too slow. also i noticed that your output is good for the basics, but you keep missing the details. eg there's more clouds, the waves are wavier and the ship has no details at all. you get the shapes right but textures and details are not there at all".

**Why:** I had told him the animator would get a "blocked-in set, not final detail" (my assumption, never confirmed by him), then showed block-ins as results. He compares the whole look of the picture: sky, water, surface detail, not only massing and camera match. Speed also failed him: pod starts without the HumGen storage, Google Drive freezing on the project folder, and every asset hand-coded from boxes.

**How to apply (my reading, not his words):** for any picture to 3D work, before showing, list sky, water, materials and small parts as differences just like shapes ([[compare-to-reference-after-stills]], [[references-carry-weight]]). If a route can only deliver shapes in the time available, say so before building and offer the detail route (textures, ready-made assets) as the choice. Do not call a block-in "done". Keep the loop fast: one asset, one picture, minutes not hours ([[say-when-stuck]]).
