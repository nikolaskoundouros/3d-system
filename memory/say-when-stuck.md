(guessed lights twice; real cause was double-sided faces in a Sketchfab model) while his actual request, a razor

**How to apply:** after one wrong guess on a side problem, say so in one line, start his requested deliverable in
parallel, and keep the diagnosis in the background. Give a time estimate with every long step.
