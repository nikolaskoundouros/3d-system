# Handoff: mom hair rebuild in Blender (work of 15 Nov 2026, written 16 Nov 2026)

## 1. What this session was doing
Rebuilding the mom's hair (from "My Mom Cracked The Dinosaur", run 021) in Blender 5.2.2 on a RunPod GPU pod, from scratch, matching her reference frames.
The method became: build hair from scripts (locks as an under-layer plus fine strands on top), put her reference frames behind solved cameras inside Blender,
render only our hair from each camera, score its silhouette against an accurate outline of her hair, and fix the biggest red/blue difference each pass.
Side view and three-quarter view are scored; the back view is not yet. A separate session ("cursor-fix") worked on making mouse brushes work; it timed out at the end.

## 2. Decisions made (with why)
- Hair is built as ONE connected mass (roots over the whole scalp, each strand blends between "swept into the bun" and "hanging"), not separate groups. Why: Nick said so; group edges showed as seams.
- Strands follow the lock paths (locks are the opaque under-layer, strands are the visible layer), colour by DIRECT RGB from her measured Lab, not melanin. Why: melanin cannot reach her golden chroma.
- Bun colour = the same hair colour as beside it. Why: measured in two lit/dark frames (t=73 s bun L 41.7 vs hair 42.3; t=16 s 11.3 vs 11.3); it only looks black in shadow.
- Compare only at MATCHED cameras (refs/cams.json). Why: earlier widths and directions were measured at mismatched angles and were invalid.
- Score against an automatic outline (outline_auto.py), not a hand-drawn polygon. Why: my polygon was a guess.
- Code builds the hair, cursor is a later add-on. Why: Comb by mouse did not work until the cursor session found the cause (see section 5).
- Pod: never end a turn with it up silently; guard stops it after 10 idle minutes (Nick's rule). It is DOWN now (checked 16 Nov 10:57).
